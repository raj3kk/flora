Flora — Privacy Policy

Ye policy Flora app aur is website dono par lagu hoti hai. Koi feature badle to ye text bhi usi ke saath update hoga — purani policy se achha chhota imaandaar text hai.

1. Jo data sirf aapke phone par rehta hai

Yaadein, Journal, Documents, Routines, Jobs, Chains, SOS contacts, Notifications aur Auto Log encrypted store me phone par save hote hain. Settings aur API keys sirf is phone par app ke private store me rehte hain, kisi server par save nahi hote aur kisi backup me nahi jaate.

2. PIN

PIN sirf AndroidKeyStore me encrypted (AES/GCM) rehta hai. PIN kabhi file, message, backup ya server par nahi likha jaata, aur kabhi log nahi hota.

3. Licence check

Jab aap Licence check dabate hain, tabhi aapka user ID aur device ID Flora ke server ko jaata hai, sirf plan/status jaanne ke liye. Licence ki key website par kabhi nahi dikhayi jaati — key sirf app me dikhti hai (masked + copy).

4. Baat karna (AI)

Jab aap Flora se baat karte hain, tabhi aapka sawaal + aakhri 20 yaadein (context ke liye) us AI provider ko jaate hain jiski key aapne Settings me daali hai (Gemini/Groq). Key na ho to Flora bina-key tools se jawab deti hai, tab kuch server par nahi jaata. Aapki poori yaadein kabhi upload nahi hoti.

5. Khoj, mausam, market, photo dekhna

Jab aap poochhte hain tabhi aapka sawaal us free service ko jaata hai (DuckDuckGo, Open-Meteo, Yahoo Finance, Tavily) — sirf utna hi jitna poochha gaya. Image banane par aapka prompt Pollinations ko jaata hai, bani hui photo sirf aapki gallery me save hoti hai. Camera se dekhi hui photo sirf tab Gemini ko jaati hai jab aap "camera dekho" ya "screen dekho" bolte hain.

6. Awaaz sunna

Mic sirf tab jab aap Mic dabate hain. Continuous mic ON ho to background me sunta hai (notification hamesha dikhegi). Wake Word ON ho to "Hey Flora" sunne ke liye mic chalu rehta hai (audio kabhi save/server par nahi jaata). Deepgram key daali ho to aapki awaaz text banane ke liye Deepgram ko jaati hai, warna system wali sunne ki suvidha chalti hai.

7. Browser

In-app browser me aap jo site kholte hain, wo site aapke phone se seedha us website ko dikhegi (jaise kisi bhi browser me). Browser history sirf is phone par rehti hai. Download aapke confirm karne par hi hota hai.

8. Push

FCM push ke liye aapka device token + device ID Flora ke server ko jaata hai (sirf tab jab aapne website account link kiya ho). Push ka text kabhi kisi teesre ko nahi becha jaata.

9. Camera aur chehra

Camera sirf Scan/Face screen par, aapke permission ke baad khulta hai. Scan ki photo sirf aapke phone ki gallery (Pictures/Flora) me save hoti hai. Face screen chehra sirf isi phone par match karti hai — photo server par kabhi nahi jaati.

10. Calls / SMS

Auto Call/SMS sirf aapke ek confirmation ke baad hote hain, aur sirf us number par jo aapne likha hai. Flora kabhi chupke se call, SMS ya post nahi karti.

11. Data kitne din rehta hai (Retention)

Chat ki yaadein phone par long-lasting rehti hain — un par 30-din ka auto-delete nahi lagta. Journal ki date wali entries, Auto Log aur dated data 30 din baad app-start par auto-delete hote hain. Bina date wali purani yaadein manually delete karni hongi (unko auto-delete kabhi nahi karega). Licence aur payment ke record (UTR, amount) alag se rakhe jaate hain.

12. Website account ka data

Website par aapka email aur payment submissions (UTR, amount, note, status) server par rehte hain — licence aur approval ke liye. Password kabhi plain text me save nahi hota (Supabase Auth ke zariye hota hai). Account se judi koi dikkat ho to support se sampark karein.

13. Bachchon ki suraksha

Flora me koi 18+ ya explicit content nahi hai. Romantic mode sirf baat karne ka tone hai, explicit baat nahi. Flora caring aur professional tareeke se baat karti hai.

14. Badlaav aur sampark

Ye policy badal sakti hai. Bada badlaav hone par app/website par bataya jayega. Sampark: flipify.com@gmail.com.
