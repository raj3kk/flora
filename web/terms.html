Flora — Terms of Use

Flora app aur is website ka istemal karne par ye shartein lagu hoti hain.

1. Plans

Free plan: roz 10 minute baat, app me maujood tools. Pro plan: ₹199/mahina — unlimited baat, sab tools, FCM push (push ke liye admin + phone ka token sync zaroori hai).

2. Licence — 1 user = 1 licence = 1 device

Ek user ki ek hi active licence hoti hai, jo ek time me ek hi device par active rehti hai. Nayi device par licence 15 din ke baad transfer hoti hai (15 din se pehle lock lagega aur transfer ki date dikhegi). Website se kharidi licence pehli verify par claim ho jaati hai. Admin device turant badal sakta hai (rebind).

3. Payment

Pro ke liye UPI se payment kijiye, phir UTR submit kijiye. UTR, amount aur note ko admin match karke approve karega — tabhi licence active hogi. App khud kabhi payment approve nahi karti. Koi payment gateway nahi hai, koi auto-charge nahi hai.

Refund ya payment se judi koi dikkat ho to support se sampark karein: flipify.com@gmail.com.

4. Aapki zimmedari

Jo number ya message aap Flora se bhejwate hain, uske liye aap zimmedar hain. Galat number par call/SMS jaane par Flora zimmedar nahi hai — confirm dialog me number dhyaan se check kijiye.

5. Galat istemal

Flora ka istemal kisi ko pareshan karne, dhokha dene, ya kanoon todne ke liye mat kijiye. Aisa karne par licence band ki ja sakti hai.

6. Availability

Flora ke jawab AI (Gemini/Groq) aur bina-key tools par depend karte hain. Network ya provider ki dikkat par jawab na mile to Flora imaandaari se bata degi, jawab gadhegi nahi.

7. Sideload app

Flora Play Store par nahi hai — ye sideload APK hai. App sirf official download page se hi install karein aur install se pehle page par likhe SHA-256 se verify karein.

8. Shikayat aur sampark

Koi shikayat ya sujhav ho to support email par sampark karein: flipify.com@gmail.com.

9. Badlaav

Ye Terms/Privacy badal sakte hain. Bada badlaav hone par app/website par bataya jayega.
