# Flora v2.17 test entry (phone par check karne ke liye)

Version: 2.17.0-rebuild, versionCode 39. CI PASS ke baad hi "done" mana jayega.

## 1. Romantic persona (user order se khula)
- "romantic mode" bolo — Flora ko pyaar bhare, caring tone me jawab
  dena chahiye. "normal mode" bolo to wapas normal tone.
- Settings me Persona list me Romantic button bhi hona chahiye.
- Romantic me koi ashleel/explicit baat nahi honi chahiye — sirf
  pyaar bhara tone hai.

## 2. Auto mode (default OFF — pehle ON karo)
- Settings me Auto mode switch ON karo (ya "auto mode on" bolo).
- ON hote hi notification me "Flora Auto mode ON" dikhna chahiye.
- "call 9876543210" bolo — bina confirm dialog ke seedha call
  lagni chahiye, aur Menu > Auto Log me entry dikhni chahiye.
- "sms 9876543210 test" aur app kholna bhi bina dialog ke hona chahiye.
- "auto mode band" bolo — notification hatni chahiye, aur agla
  call phir se confirm dialog ke saath aana chahiye.
- Auto Log screen: har auto kaam date/time ke saath dikhna chahiye;
  "Log saaf karo" se saaf hona chahiye.

## 3. Jo kabhi auto nahi hoga (check karo ki ye sach hai)
- Payment ka step kabhi auto nahi hona chahiye — Pro khareedne
  par bhi UTR/admin approval wala raasta hi rahega.

## Phone par FAIL ho to
- Kaunsa step ruka, Flora ne kya bola, Auto Log me kya likha —
  ye teeno batao.
